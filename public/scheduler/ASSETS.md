# Scheduler artwork assets

- `../fonts/Anton-Regular.ttf`: Google Fonts, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton . SIL Open Font License, copied to `../fonts/Anton-OFL.txt`.
- `../fonts/Oswald-Variable.ttf`: Google Fonts, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oswald . SIL Open Font License, copied to `../fonts/Oswald-OFL.txt`.
- Player photos and club crests are fetched from the existing Premier League image source through the local image proxy. They are selected by player photo code and club code, never by list position or random selection.

Artwork layouts, lettering, statistics, colours, SVG backgrounds and transfer bars are rendered from code. No stadium photographs are used. The generated design-reference sheets are not used as backgrounds or baked into the exported posts.
